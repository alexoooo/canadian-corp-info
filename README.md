# canadian-corp-info
Information about corporate tax, accounting, and other paperwork.  

This info is intended for small business (Canadian-Controlled Private Corporation, CCPC) owners to help with paperwork and tax filing.  

Over the years of book-keeping and filing taxes, certain questions keep coming up, and there are many conflicting and incomplete answeres on the internet.  
This website is my attempt at consolidating an understanding of how to properly handle these different situations.

Disclaimer: I am not an accountant, this is not finanical or tax advice.
