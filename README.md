# Canadian Corporation Information

Information about corporate tax, accounting, and other paperwork.  

Target audience: small business (Canadian-Controlled Private Corporation, CCPC) owners and operators.

Over the years of bookkeeping and tax filing, certain questions recur, and there are many conflicting and incomplete answers on the internet.  
This guide is an attempt to consolidate practical guidance on how to handle these different situations reasonably.  

Disclaimer: author is not an accountant, this is not financial or tax advice.
