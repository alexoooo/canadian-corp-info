When you have a corporate investment account holding an ETF which pays distributions (eg. XEI), you will see the following:
- Box 49: Actual amount of eligible dividends
- Box 21: Capital gains
- Box 25: Foreign income
- Box 34: Foreign tax withheld
- Box 42: Return of capital (ROC)
- Box 26: Other income

Throughout the year the distirbutions arrive in your investment account, but we won't know the final tax classification until we get the T3.
With RBC Direct Investing this typically happens in the 2nd half of March, and might require a call to get them to email the T3 (and other) investment package PDFs.
