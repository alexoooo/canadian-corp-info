## Repo-wide findings

### [HIGH] R-1 ERDTOH/NERDTOH attributed to s.89(1) instead of s.129(4)

ERDTOH and NERDTOH are defined in **ITA s.129(4)**; s.89(1) defines GRIP, LRIP, CDA, "eligible
dividend", PUC, and the 0.72 general-rate factor — not the RDTOH pools. The misattribution appears at:

- `Dividends/ERDTOH-NERDTOH.md:52` (body) — "ERDTOH … and NERDTOH … are defined in ITA s.89(1)"
- `Dividends/ERDTOH-NERDTOH.md:246` (Citations) — "s.89(1) - definitions of *ERDTOH* and *NERDTOH*"
- `T3/T3.md:259` (Citations) — "s.89(1) - definitions of ERDTOH, NERDTOH, GRIP, and capital dividend account"
- `Concept-Map.md:389` (Citations) — "s.89(1) - GRIP, CDA, ERDTOH, NERDTOH definitions and the 0.72 … factor"

All three pages are signed off (authoritative), and each cites s.129(4) correctly elsewhere
(`ERDTOH-NERDTOH.md:250`, `T3.md:138`, `Concept-Map.md:392`), so each is internally inconsistent.
Source: laws-lois s.129(4) ("The definitions in this subsection apply in this section") contains
"eligible refundable dividend tax on hand" and "non-eligible refundable dividend tax on hand"; s.89(1)
does not. Fix: move ERDTOH/NERDTOH to a s.129(4) citation on each page; keep GRIP/CDA/0.72 under
s.89(1).

### [MEDIUM] R-2 "Cost is in the UCC pool from the acquisition date" contradicts s.13(26)

- `Cost-Recovery/Cost-Recovery.md:198` and `Cost-Recovery/Capital-Cost-Allowance/Capital-Cost-Allowance.md:131` — "The cost is in the UCC pool from the acquisition date …"

s.13(26) excludes the capital cost from UCC until the property is available for use; the cost is *not*
in the pool from acquisition. Both pages contradict their own correct statements one or two lines away
(`Cost-Recovery.md:197`; `Capital-Cost-Allowance.md:57` "cost of additions … that are available for
use (s.13(26))"). The no-CCA-until-available conclusion is right; the mechanism is mis-described, and
a reader could overstate a Schedule 8 addition or opening UCC. Source: ITA s.13(26) (fetched) — "no
amount shall be included in calculating the undepreciated capital cost … before the time the property
is considered to have become available for use." Fix: "the cost does not enter the class's UCC until
the property is available for use (s.13(26))."

### [MEDIUM] R-3 GIFI 1900 used for "Machinery and equipment" (should be 1740)

- `Capital-Cost-Allowance.md:279` — "`Machinery and equipment` 1900"
- `CCA-Examples.md:82-83` — Class 8 floor polisher posted to "`Machinery and equipment - cost` (GIFI 1900)"
- `Cost-Recovery.md:242` — lists 1900 in the fixed-asset code set

GIFI **1740** is "Machinery, equipment, furniture and fixtures"; **1900** is "Other tangible capital
assets" (art, books, culverts, dams, towers, …). The guide's own `Ledger-And-Accounts.md:204` uses
1740 correctly, and `CCA-Tracking.md` books the same polisher to 1740 — so the 1900 usage is both
wrong against RC4088 and internally inconsistent. Source: RC4088 GIFI (search index). Fix: post
machinery/equipment to 1740; reserve 1900 for genuine other-tangible assets.

### [MEDIUM] BAS-1 T5 late penalty stated "per slip"

- `Bookkeeping-And-Slips.md:90` — "$10 per day per slip"

The s.162(7.01) penalty is a single charge per information-return *type*, not per slip: for under 51

slip count. The bracketed $100–$1,000 range is right; the "per slip" rate is wrong. Source: laws-lois
s.162(7.01) (fetched). Fix: "$10 per day for the whole T5 filing (not per slip), minimum $100, capped
at 100 days."

### [MEDIUM] T2R-1 EEDD paragraph (c) attributed to the "GRIP definition"

### [HIGH] CDA-1 s.83(2) election deadline stated as "payment date"

- `Capital-Dividend-Account.md:40`, `:89` — "submit Form T2054 … on or before the payment date"

s.83(2) fixes the deadline at the day the dividend **becomes payable** (the date the directors' resolution
sets) "or the first day on which any part of the dividend was paid if that day is earlier." "Payment

Source: laws-lois s.83(2) (fetched, verbatim). Fix: "the day the dividend becomes payable (or the
first day any part is paid, if earlier)."

### [HIGH] FC-1 "Most service consultants are ineligible for the Quick Method" is wrong

- `Foreign-Currency.md:355` — "(most service consultants are ineligible for Quick Method regardless of the zero-rating)"

The Quick Method ineligible list is narrow: legal, accounting, actuarial, bookkeeping, financial-

are eligible, so "most are ineligible" is false. The intended point is the *zero-rating* one — an
all-non-resident-client consultant gains no *benefit* (the remittance % collapses to zero on zero-rated
supplies), which `HST.md:253` states correctly. It also contradicts the HST page's own IT-consulting
CCPC running on the Quick Method (Example 2, `:407-449`) and the maintainer's own situation. Source:
CRA RC4058 ineligible-designations list (search index). Fix: replace with "an all-non-resident-client
consultant gains no benefit from the Quick Method because zero-rated supplies carry no HST to keep."

### [MEDIUM] HST-1 "4.2% of GST/HST-inclusive revenue" overstates the kept spread

- `HST.md:251` — "the kept spread (4.2% of GST/HST-inclusive revenue …)"

4.2% is 13% − 8.8%, subtracting a rate-on-net from a rate-on-inclusive. HST collected is 13/113 =
11.504% of inclusive revenue; the Quick Method remits 8.8% of inclusive, so the true spread is ≈2.70%
of inclusive (≈3.7% on the first $30k after the 1% credit). The page's own Example 2 gives $1,675.20 ÷
$50,850 = 3.29%. Fix: state ≈2.7% of inclusive (≈3.0% of net).

- `HST.md:439` — close credits "HST remittance payable = $4,174.80" but the actual remittance is $3,654.80 (`:432-433`), and the $520 capital ITC in `HST receivable` (`:418`) is never cleared

### [MEDIUM] HST-3 Registration effective-date rule omits the four-quarter grace; s.240(2.1) mis-attached

- `HST.md:37-40` — presents the single-quarter rule (effective date = the crossing supply, taxable) as universal, and attaches the s.240(2.1) cite to the effective-date bullet

For the four-quarter test (s.148(1)) the person stays a small supplier through the following month and
the crossing supply is *not* taxable; s.240(2.1) governs the *application deadline*, not the effective
date. Source: laws-lois ETA s.148(1)/(2), s.240(2.1) (fetched). Fix: split the two tests; move the
s.240(2.1) cite to the application-deadline bullet.

### [MEDIUM] HST-4 ETA s.148 subsection citations are wrong

### [MEDIUM] HST-6 Quick-vs-regular net-benefit ignores the expense gross-up

- `HST.md:446-447` — taxes the full $1,675.20 spread (~$204) and reports "~$1,159 better"

Under the Quick Method the operating inputs are expensed gross ($312 more deductible than the regular

saved), and the net benefit is $1,363.20 × (1 − 0.122) ≈ **$1,197**, not ~$1,159. Fix: tax the net
income difference (or state ≈ cash saving × (1 − ABI rate)).
