## New findings

### [CRITICAL] C-1 Wrong Part IV rate and miscategorisation in T3.md

`T3.md:142` reads:
> Source: Part IV Tax on non-eligible dividends received from other corporations (25% rate for connected corporations)

Two errors compound in one parenthetical:

1. **Rate**. 25% is not the current Part IV rate. Per ITA
   [s.186(1)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-3.3/section-186.html), the Part IV rate
   is **38⅓% of all assessable dividends received from corporations not connected with the
   recipient** (verified live).
2. **Connection direction**. 38⅓% applies to *non-connected* dividends. For *connected*
   corporations Part IV tax is *not* a flat percentage — it equals the proportion of the payer's
   dividend refund attributable to the dividend received by the recipient (s.186(1)(b)). The
   bracket reverses the relationship.

`T3.md:144` then says:
> Source: Part IV Tax on eligible portfolio dividends received (38⅓% rate; this is the mechanism that funds the 38⅓% dividend refund)

The 38⅓% rate is correct, but the framing implies the rate depends on whether the dividend
received is eligible vs non-eligible. It does not. The rate depends on connected-vs-non-connected;
the *destination* of the Part IV tax (ERDTOH for eligible received, NERDTOH for non-eligible
received) is what depends on the dividend type, per the s.129(4) definitions of ERDTOH and
NERDTOH.

Suggested fix: restructure the two NERDTOH/ERDTOH source bullets to first state the Part IV rate
once ("38⅓% on portfolio (non-connected) dividends; flow-through on connected dividends per
s.186(1)") and then say which pool receives the resulting tax based on the type of dividend
received. The existing Citations entry at `T3.md:266` (s.186) can be linked inline.

### [MEDIUM] M-1 T3.md confuses ACB and Book Cost in the brokerage paragraph

`T3.md:40`:
> All brokerages might occasionally forget to properly adjust the Book Cost  (e.g. with RBC if you sell the security before April) and sometimes report the wrong amount (which can be resolved by raising the issue with the brokerage), and they might even not update the ACB at all.

The paragraph is about the *brokerage's* own internal numbers. Brokerages track Book Cost; ACB
is the CRA-defined value the *taxpayer* tracks. The whole rest of the document (and `ACB.md`,
and `T5008.md`) hammers exactly that distinction. The trailing "they might even not update the
ACB at all" smuggles "ACB" back into a brokerage-side discussion.

Suggested fix: replace the trailing clause with "they might not update the Book Cost at all" and
collapse the duplicate-information run-on into two sentences. (Pairs with L-2 below.)

### [MEDIUM] M-2 ACB.md identical-property folio citation is the wrong folio

`Adjusted-Cost-Base.md:130-133` (and the matching Citations entry at L158):
> What is the action: acquisition of an identical property — same issuer, same class, same currency of issue (ITA [s.248(12)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-3.3/section-248.html); CRA Folio S3-F3-C1); whether two different ETFs tracking the same index are identical is fact-specific

CRA Folio **S3-F3-C1 is not on point** — that folio is *Replacement Property* under ITA s.13(4)
and s.44(1), an unrelated regime. The "same issuer, class, currency-of-issue" criteria are
articulated in CRA's archived **IT-387R2** (*Meaning of "identical properties"*) and the case law
summarised there. The cifinancial article already cited in `Links` covers the same ground — its
on-page summary calls the test a "question of fact" with properties identical when "the same in
all material respects, so that a prospective buyer would not have a preference for one as opposed
to another."

The s.248(12) citation could not be re-verified live (the laws-lois page returned only s.248(1)
content via WebFetch). Verify whether s.248(12) actually articulates the "same issuer / class /
currency" criteria; if it does not, drop it in favour of IT-387R2.

Suggested fix: replace the S3-F3-C1 reference with `(IT-387R2 (Archived))`, double-check the
s.248(12) citation against the statutory text, and update the Citations block at L158 in
lockstep.
