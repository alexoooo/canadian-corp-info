## What this covers

A corporation is a separate legal person with a life of its own: it is created, it holds a structure of shares and roles while it operates, it may buy or reorganize a business along the way, and eventually it is dissolved. This group collects the pages tied to those lifecycle moments, as opposed to the recurring year-to-year work covered under Operations and Filing.

- [Corporate Structure](Corporate-Structure/Corporate-Structure.md) is the standing picture: the corporation as a separate legal person, the three roles (shareholders, directors, officers), federal versus provincial incorporation, the minute book, and the [share capital](Corporate-Structure/Share-Capital.md) that ownership is divided into.
- [Starting Up](Starting-Up.md) covers the choices made at incorporation and the first-year setup.
- [Business Acquisition](Business-Acquisition/Business-Acquisition.md) is the orientation for buying or restructuring a business — the [asset-versus-share](Business-Acquisition/Asset-vs-Share.md) fork, paying with [preferred-share consideration](Business-Acquisition/Preferred-Share-Consideration.md), and the [estate freeze](Business-Acquisition/Estate-Freeze.md).
- [Winding Down](Winding-Down.md) covers dissolution and the final filings that close the corporation out.
