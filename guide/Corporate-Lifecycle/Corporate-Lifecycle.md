## Sub-Pages

A corporation is a separate legal person with a life of its own.  
It is created, it holds a structure of shares and roles while it operates, and eventually it is dissolved.  
Along the way it may buy or reorganize a business.  
This group collects the pages tied to those lifecycle moments.  
The recurring year-to-year work lives under [Operations](../Operations/Operations.md) and [Filing and CRA](../Filing-And-CRA/Filing-And-CRA.md).
