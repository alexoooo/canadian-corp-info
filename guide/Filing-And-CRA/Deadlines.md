# Deadlines

**Who this is for**:
- Owners of a Canadian-controlled private corporation (CCPC) checking a CRA or registry due date

**TLDR**:
- Corporate tax runs on two clocks: the balance is paid 2-3 months after year-end, the return is filed at 6 months
- GST/HST and the registry follow the fiscal year; slips and payroll remittances follow the calendar

Limitations:
- A lookup calendar only: the tests, computations, and payment mechanics live on the linked pages
- Owner-manager CCPC scope; the registry rows cover federal (CBCA) and Ontario corporations
- Dates as of 2026


## Corporate Tax

| Obligation | Due | Details |
|---|---|---|
| T2 balance | 2 months after year-end; 3 for a qualifying CCPC | [Payment](Payment/Payment.md#corporate-income-tax) |
| T2 return | 6 months after year-end | [T2 Schedules](T2-Schedules.md) |
| Instalments | Monthly; quarterly for an eligible small CCPC | [Payment](Payment/Payment.md#instalment-computation) |
| T1135 foreign-property report | With the T2, 6 months after year-end | [T1135](../Investments/T1135.md) |

- The balance is due before the return: estimate and pay by month 2 or 3, file by month 6
- The 3-month balance-due day takes the full CCPC/SBD/preceding-year test on [Payment](Payment/Payment.md#corporate-income-tax)
- Monthly instalments are due the last day of each month; quarterly, the last day of each fiscal quarter
- No instalments while total tax payable, or the first instalment base, is $3,000 or less

## Payroll and Slips

| Obligation | Due | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Source-deduction remittance | 15th of the month after the pay | [Payment](Payment/Payment.md#payroll-remittance) |
| Nil remittance (no-salary period) | Same due date as the remittance it replaces | [Payment](Payment/Payment.md#payroll-remittance) |
| T4 slips and summary | Last day of February after the calendar year | [Payroll](../Paying-Yourself/Payroll.md) |
| T5 slips and summary | Last day of February after the calendar year | [Bookkeeping and Slips](../Paying-Yourself/Dividends/Bookkeeping-And-Slips.md) |

- The 15th-of-following-month remittance is the *regular* (monthly) remitter cadence, the owner-manager default
- Slip penalties run per return type even when no tax is owed; see [CRA Administration](CRA-Administration.md)
