## The Year in One Diagram

```mermaid
flowchart TB
    Corp["The corporation:<br/>T4 and T5 slips"]
    Other["Other slips:<br/>T3, T5, T5008, RRSP receipts"]
    AFR["CRA account:<br/>Auto-fill my return"]
    T1["T1 return"]
    Credits["Credits:<br/>donations, medical"]
    Pay["Balance due Apr 30<br/>filing Apr 30 / Jun 15"]
    Room["TFSA / RRSP room:<br/>contribute, deduct"]

    Corp --> AFR
    Other --> AFR
    AFR --> T1
    Credits --> T1
    Room --> T1
    T1 --> Pay
```

Salary feeds next year's RRSP room; dividends do not.  
The room track runs on its own calendar (60 days after year-end for the RRSP deduction, January 1 for TFSA room).
